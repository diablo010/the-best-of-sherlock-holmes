ADVENTURE OF THE DANCING MEN


A letter was sent to Holmes which contained childish drawings. A Norfolk squire named Mr Hilton Cubitt had approached Holmes since his wife was depressed by a letter. Elsie Patrick, an American young lady married to Hilton but before that she told she didn't wish to recall her past. The terror-creating letters with strange drawings have perturbed their life. A chain of these dancing men was telegrammed to Holmes. Holmes and Watson departed to meet the Norfolk squire and inform him about the progress. After reaching his residence, they realised that Mr Cubitt was shot dead and Mrs Cubitt was in critical condition.

When Holmes along with inspector Martin investigated in that room, a pistol with two barrels emptied was found. The two maids of their house were questioned when they told that there were repeated noise and smell of gunpowder and smoke had filled the passage. Also, the doors were fastened from inside. They added that Cubitts never quarelled and were known to be united. Holmes again examined the room to see that a bullet was struck at the edge of the window. Beneath the window, there was a trampled flowerbed and among the grass lied the third cartridge. He asked a lad to take a note to Mr Abe Slaney at the Elrige's farm.

Holmes revealed the fact that he had encrypted the "dancing men" a script where a dangerous crook forced Elsie to come to that room. To which Elsie affirmated if he would never return thenafter. But,unfortunately, Mr Cubitt interfered and he and the crook shot towards each other where the squire was killed and then, Elsie attempted suicide by shootin herself as she was the cause of squire's death. At that time, a gentleman came over and asked for Elsie when Holmes told that she is about to die. The young American then, confessed that he and Elsie worked together for the notorious JOINT gang. But, Elsie abhorred this and she left by putting a condition before me that she would marry him only if he quits the job. He was enraged when she got married and out of fury, he tried to kill and accidentally shot the squire. And that's how the squire met an unfortunate death.
