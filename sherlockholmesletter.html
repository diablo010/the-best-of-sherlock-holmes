From

Mr Hilton Cubitt

33,Norfolk

London

To

Mr Sherlock Holmes

221B,Baker Street

London

Dear Sir

From the past few weeks, pieces of paper with some childish drawings have depressed Mrs Cubitt. Everything was fine until she received a letter from America consisting of strange figures which has terrified her. She has been frequently receiving the dancing figures making her restless. I have enclosed that paper with this letter. I look forward for your asistance.

Yours sincerely

Mr Hilton Cubitt
