THE SPECKLED BAND


A client named Helen Stoner approached Mr Sherlock Holmes to seek help. Holmes meticulously briefed her how she must have travelled to arrive at Baker street, 221B. This gesture assured Miss Stoner of some help from Holmes. She lived with her stepfather who was doctor by profession and had a strange passion for Indian animals and even petted a baboon and a cheetah at home. Mrs Stoner had provised a sum of $1000 a year on the event of Helen's and her twin Julia's marriage. But before the day of marriage, Miss Stoner's sister died a strange unexpected death. Miss Stoner also mentioned of some whistles since past few days before her sister's death. Hence, Miss Stoner was worried as she was about to marry and feared to be destined as same as her twin. After Miss Stoner left Baker Street, her stepfather arrived who had actually traced and advised Holmes not to interfere.

Holmes and Watson then arrived at the Roylott's and spectated the rooms where her sister slept and now she was told to sleep and her stepfather's. Holmes found it peculiar that there was a ventilator which opened into her sister's room. Also,there was an iron safe which striked strange to him. In her sister's room what strange was the bed fixed down the ventilator and a dummy bell rope. Holmes devised to come at midnight and stay in that room.

Accordingly, they entered the room and switched the lights off. Suddenly, they smelt cigar through ventilator. Some time later, Holmes impromptu striked against the ventilator hard and until Watson could realise there was a deafening shriek from the stepfather's room. They hurriedly rushed to see a speckled band round the Doctor's head. Holmes explained that the cunning doctor murdered his stepdauhter with the snake whose poison could not even be detected. He used to send serpent from the ventilator and with the support of dummy bell rope, it crawled down the ventilator and bit Miss Stoner's sister. Then, the trained serpent used to return after doctor whistled. Today when it arrived there, Holmes striked against the ventilator and out of fury he attacked the first person he saw, Dr Roylott. The doctor used to keep this venomous in the iron safe. That's how Dr Roylott murdered Miss Stoner's sister.
