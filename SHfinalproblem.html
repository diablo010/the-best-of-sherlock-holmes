THE FINAL PROBLEM


Holmes had disappeared since his last meeting with Professor Moriarity. Before this tragedy, Holmes once came to Watson's house and with his permission, Holmes shut the windows. He was quite paler and thinner than usual. then, Holmes spoke about Professor Moriarity that he was an excellent mathematician and worked in one of the universities. But, the diabolical strain ran in his blood which rendered him infinitely. Dark rumors spread which made him resign his chair and now, he has set up as an army coach. He is the mastermind of the criminal world or the Napoleon of crime. Hence, Holmes devoted completely in order to expose him. Holmes toiled very hard to collect evidences against him.

That's why he came to Watson's house by climbing the wall as the Moriarity's men kept a watch on his movements. Many times, his men tried to attack Holmes. So, he devised to tell Watson everything in a reserved compartment of train. Moriarity was rightly following them. Holmes told Watson that they have to think like Moriarity. Accordingly, he would be waiting at the luggage pick-up for Watson. Thence, we must live for the hotel.

It had been many months since Holmes and Watson left England. At every minute, Holmes used to glance back at strangers. Once, Holmes and Watson went for a walk over the abyss when a Swiss lad came with a note to guve to Watson which read that an Englishwoman at the last stage of consumption at tie hotel wishes to see an English doctor. Watson was reluctant to go but Holmes asked him to go. When Watson reached the hotel, the owner told there wasn't any sick Englishwoman and the note was written by the Englishman who went after Holmes and Watson. Then, Watson ran towards the abyss where he found the trail of two men till the end of the cliff. Also, a letter kept under silver cigarette box over a rock. The letter read that Holmes knew that the note was hoax and he didn't care to lose his life to protect the society. And from that day, Holmes was believed to be dead.
