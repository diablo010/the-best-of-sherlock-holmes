A SCANDAL IN BOHEMIA


To Sherlock Holmes, SHE is always the woman. The woman was late Irene Adler, of dubious and questionable memory. A note had been delivered to Holmes from anonymous. After studying the texture and initials woven into the texture of paper, Holmes told Watson that he must be German from the accent used and the paper was manufactured in Bohemia. Shortly after the letter was received, a masked man entered and addressed himself as the Count Von Kramm, a Bohemian nobleman and spoke about that matter implicating to the great house of Ormstein, hereditary kings of Bohemia. Whan Holmes address him as Majesty, he revealed his identity as a king. The king informed them about a photo which was in the profession of a beautiful and witty lady named Irene Adler. She is trying to blackmail him by revealing it to his bride's family.

Holmes spectated around the residence of Miss Adler and also enquired in the neighbourhood. The lady sings at the concert and returns sharp at seven. The only visitor she had was Godfrey Norton, a lawyer. Miss Adler and Mr Norton rushed to the church where Holmes followed them. Suddenly, Mr Norton rushed towards Holmes and dragged him to the altar. Holmes realised that he was a witness to their marriage as the clergyman absloutely refused to let them marry without a witness. Then, they parted on their ways after departing from the church. Holmes devised a plan to make her reveal the photograph. He dressed himself as an innocent clergyman and asked Watson to raise a 'fire' alarm. Holmes reached before Miss Adler arrived and pretended to be injured so Miss Adler offered help and that's how he entered Miss Adler's sitting room. As soon as Holmes signalled, Watson threw the smoke-rocket and raised the false 'fire' alarm. Then, Holmes assured that it was false. Till the alarm was raised, Miss Adler reached towards the drawer to take photograph. No sooner did she realise, he went off from there.

Next day, Holmes and Watson along with the king to the lounge. The maid informed them that Miss Adler had left England along with Mr Norton permanently. Holmes rushed to peep into the drawer to find that photograph,however,Miss Adler had left her photograph of hers in an evening dress and a letter addressed to Holmes where she wrote about the recognition of Mr Holmes in clergy's disguise and further assured no interference in king's life. Afterwards, Holmes asked the Bohemian king to let him keep the photograph.
